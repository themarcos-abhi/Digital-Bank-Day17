# Capstone Ticket Backlog

## A: Consultation down for all evening patients
Incident, P1 candidate due to broad critical-journey impact. Validate scope and business impact, check service/host/dependencies/logs and recent changes, publish frequent fact-based updates, restore or escalate.

## B: New provider login
Service request, normally P3 subject to the agreed matrix. Validate requester authorization and approval, create through the approved identity process, share activation securely, record audit evidence.

## C: Reports slow while booking works
Incident, likely P2/P3 depending measured impact. Compare service-specific latency, database/log timings, host metrics and recent deployments. Do not call it a database issue without evidence.

## D: Intermittent notifications four times this week
Create/link incidents to a problem record. Correlate timestamps with the recurring background job, then examine logs, queue/dependency response and correlation IDs before proposing cause. Escalate with a timeline and sanitized evidence.

## E: Vault-password phishing email
Security incident. Do not click, reply, forward externally or disclose credentials. Preserve using the approved reporting method, notify the security channel, follow containment directions and rotate credentials only when directed by the authorized security process.
